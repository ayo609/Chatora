FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about Chatora.

Is Chatora free to use?+
Yes. Chatora is completely free for personal use. Create an account, chat with friends, send voice notes, and use the AI features — all without paying. No hidden charges.
How is Chatora different from WhatsApp?+
Chatora adds an AI layer on top of standard messaging. You get chat summarization (catch up on missed messages instantly), real-time translation across languages, and smart reply suggestions — features WhatsApp doesn't offer.
Is my data safe?+
Your messages and media are stored securely on Google Firebase infrastructure with authentication and access rules enforced. Only you and the people you chat with can see your conversations.
Can I send voice messages?+
Yes. Hold the microphone button in any chat to record. Release to send. Your voice note is uploaded securely and delivered instantly to the recipient, who can play it inline.
What languages does the AI translate?+
Chatora's translation supports major world languages plus Nigerian languages including Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo. More languages are being added regularly.
Do I need to download an app?+
No download required. Chatora runs entirely in your web browser — on phone, tablet, or desktop. Just open the site and sign in.
Can I create group chats?+
Yes. Create unlimited group chats for friends, family, study groups, or teams. Add or remove members anytime. Group admins have full control over membership.
How does the AI summarizer work?+
When you tap "Summarize", Chatora sends the recent messages in that chat to our AI engine, which analyzes the conversation and returns a concise summary highlighting decisions, action items, and key points.
Will Chatora work on slow internet?+
Yes. Chatora is built to be lightweight. Messages are optimized for minimal data usage, and Firebase handles reconnection gracefully — so you stay connected even on unstable networks.
Can I delete my account?+
Yes. You can delete your account at any time from Settings. All your personal data, messages, and media will be permanently removed from our systems.
Do you support video or voice calls?+
Voice and video calling are on our product roadmap. Chatora 1.0 focuses on text, voice messages, media sharing, and AI-powered features — with calling arriving in a future release.
How do I report a problem or send feedback?+
Head to Settings → Help & Feedback inside the app, or email our team directly. We read every message and use feedback to improve Chatora continuously.
