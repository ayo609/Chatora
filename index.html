FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about Chatora.

Is Chatora free to use?+
Yes — completely free for personal use. Create an account, chat with friends, send voice notes, and use the AI features without paying.
How is Chatora different from other chat apps?+
Chatora adds an AI layer on top of standard messaging — summarization, real-time translation, and smart reply suggestions built directly into every conversation.
Is my data safe?+
Your messages and media are secured with modern authentication and access rules. Only you and the people you chat with can see your conversations.
Can I send voice messages?+
Yes. Hold the microphone button in any chat to record. Release to send. Your voice note is delivered instantly and plays inline.
What languages does the AI translate?+
Major world languages plus Nigerian languages including Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo. More being added regularly.
Do I need to download an app?+
No download. Chatora runs in your browser on phone, tablet, or desktop. Just open and sign in.
Can I create group chats?+
Yes — unlimited groups for friends, family, study, or teams. Add or remove members anytime. Admins have full control.
Will it work on slow internet?+
Yes. Chatora is built to be lightweight with graceful reconnection, so you stay connected even on unstable networks.
Can I delete my account?+
Yes. Delete from Settings at any time. All your data is permanently removed.
Do you support voice or video calls?+
Voice and video calling are on the roadmap. Chatora 1.0 focuses on text, voice messages, media, and AI-powered features — with calling coming in a future release.
